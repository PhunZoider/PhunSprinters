description=
description=PhunSprinters is the ultimate dynamic sprinter experience for Zomboid. PhunSprinters does [b]not[/b] simply add sprinters.
description=It calculates [b]risk[/b] and that risk can change depending on [b]where you are, how long you’ve survived, the moon cycle, and the environment[/b].
description=
description=[h1]Why not just turn on vanilla sprinters?[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Vanilla gives you a percentage. Those zeds are sprinters everywhere, at all times, from your first hour to your last.
description=
description=PhunSprinters makes it situational. Risk climbs the longer you survive, shifts with the moon, and changes when you cross into a different area. Depending on the mode you pick, the same zed can shamble past you at noon and come at a dead run after dark.
description=
description=Then there is light. With suppression on, a sprinter caught in enough light drops back to a walk, which turns your torch from something you leave on into a decision you make.
description=
description=[h1]How risk is calculated[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Sprinter behaviour is determined by stacking modifiers in this order:
description=[list]
description=[*]Global Base Risk (in your settings)
description=[*]Zone Override (when [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3676252660]PhunZones 2[/url] is installed). [url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#zone-recipes]Recipes for safe havens and no-go areas[/url]
description=[*]How long you've played for
description=[*]What phase the moon is in
description=[*]The mode you use (see below)
description=[*]Light Suppression (enable to slow sprinters in light)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#how-risk-is-calculated]The exact formula, with a worked example[/url]
description=
description=[h1]Modes[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=There are four different modes that determine when sprinters will run
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Mode[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]1- Dusk till Dawn[/b][/td][td]Sprinters will walk during daytime, but when the sun goes down, they run[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]2- Always[/b][/td][td]Sprinters will always run (like vanilla)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]3- When its too dark outside[/b][/td][td]During the night or when weather reduces visibility, zeds will run (even if its daytime).[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]4- When its too dark around them[/b][/td][td]This is the phunest option, but can be the most demanding performance wise. In this mode, sprinters will run if it ever gets too dark around them. In this mode, your flashlight becomes a critical part of gameplay.[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=[h1]Light suppression[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=When enabled, light forces sprinters to slow down
description=
description=This creates emergent gameplay:
description=[list]
description=[*]Flashlights become tactical tools
description=[*]Base lighting matters
description=[*]Night travel becomes a risk vs reward decision
description=[*]It brings some of the Phun of mode 4 to modes 1, 2 and 3. Mode 4 already works this way, so the setting does nothing there
description=[*]It applies to every sprinter in the world, including ones the game rolled itself
description=[/list]
description=
description=Some limits exist to prevent performance issues in dense areas.
description=
description=[url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#light-suppression]How the light level is measured, indoors and out[/url]
description=
description=[h1]Hours discount[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Ramps difficulty with time survived instead of throwing full risk at a fresh character. A new player can be seeing a fraction of an area's risk while a veteran standing next to them takes all of it.
description=
description=Ideal for fresh servers, and for keeping someone's first night survivable without softening the map for everyone else.
description=
description=[url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#hours-discount]The ramp, hour by hour[/url]
description=
description=[h1]Moon phase modifiers[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Moon phases [b]multiply[/b] risk. A full moon night can be twice as dangerous as a new moon one, and every phase is configurable.
description=
description=Set a phase to 0 and there are no sprinters at all on those nights, which turns a new moon into a window you can plan a run around.
description=
description=[url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#moon-phases]Every phase and its default[/url]

description=[h1]Using this with the vanilla Zombie Speed setting[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=PhunSprinters has to own who runs, so it takes vanilla's sprinters off the board first.
description=
description=If your Zombie Speed is [b]Sprinters[/b], those zeds are converted to a slower speed when the world loads, and PhunSprinters decides which of them run from there. If it is [b]Random[/b], only the sprinter share is removed, so your fast shambler and shambler mix is left as it is. On [b]Fast Shamblers[/b] or [b]Shamblers[/b] nothing is changed at all.
description=
description=This is what makes everything above work. A vanilla sprinter used to ignore light suppression, dusk till dawn and zone limits completely, because there was no way to slow one down.
description=
description=Want every zed running, the way vanilla does it? Set Default Risk to 100 and Mode to Always.
description=
description=Nothing is written to your save. The change applies for the session only.
description=
