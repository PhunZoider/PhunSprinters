title=PhunSprinters 2
description=[b]PhunSprinters 2[/b] the sequel to the Academy Award winning [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3532685233]PhunSprinters[/url].
description=
description=PhunSprinters is the ultimate sprinter mod for Zomboid. PhunSprinters does [b]not[/b] simply add sprinters.
description=It calculates [b]risk[/b] and that risk can change depending on [b]where you are, how long you’ve survived, the moon cycle, and the environment[/b].
description=
description=[h1]Read this if you run Zombie Speed on Sprinters[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=PhunSprinters now decides who sprints, and the game's own sprinter setting no longer does.
description=
description=If your Zombie Speed is set to [b]Sprinters[/b], every zed used to run and the mod could not do anything about it. They are now converted to a slower base speed when the world loads, and only the zeds PhunSprinters promotes will run, when your mode and light rules say they should.
description=
description=[b]To keep the old behaviour, set Default Risk to 100 and Mode to Always.[/b]
description=
description=Every other setting is left alone. On [b]Fast Shamblers[/b] or [b]Shamblers[/b] nothing changes at all, because there were no vanilla sprinters to replace. On [b]Random[/b] only the sprinter share is removed, so your existing fast shambler and shambler mix is untouched.
description=
description=Nothing is written to your save. The change applies for the session only, so removing the mod gives you your settings back exactly as they were.
description=
description=[h1]How sprinter risk is calculated[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Sprinter behaviour is determined by stacking modifiers in this order:
description=[list]
description=[*]Global Base Risk (in your settings)
description=[*]Zone Override (if PhunZones 2 is installed)
description=[*]Hours Discount (Ease-In)
description=[*]Moon Phase Modifier
description=[*]Darkness / Night Rules
description=[*]Light Suppression
description=[*]Final Sprinter Chance & Behaviour
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h1]Modes[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=There are four different modes that determine when sprinters will run
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Mode[/th][th]Description[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Dusk till Dawn[/b][/td][td]Sprinters will walk during daytime, but when the sun goes down, they run[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]Always [/b][/td][td]Sprinters will always run[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]When its too dark outside[/b][/td][td]During the night or when weather reduces visibility, zeds will run (even if its daytime).[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]When its too dark around them[/b][/td][td]This is the phunest option, but least performant. In this mode, sprinters will run if it ever gets too dark around them. In this mode, your flashlight becomes a critical part of gameplay.[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=[h1]Light suppression[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=When enabled, bright light can force sprinters to walk
description=
description=This creates emergent gameplay:
description=[list]
description=[*]Flashlights become tactical tools
description=[*]Base lighting matters
description=[*]Night travel becomes a risk vs reward decision
description=[*]It brings some of the Phun of mode #4 to the other options (this option doesn't do anything if using Mode 4)
description=[/list]
description=
description=Some limits exist to prevent performance issues in dense areas.
description=
description=[h1]Hours discount[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=The [b]Hours Discount[/b] slowly ramps difficulty over time.
description=Instead of applying full risk immediately, sprinter chance increases gradually.
description=
description=Example:
description=[list]
description=[*]Zone Risk: 50%
description=[*]Hours Discount: 100
description=[*]Player Hours: 50
description=[/list]
description=
description=Effective risk = 25%
description=
description=Risk increases linearly until the discount hours are reached.
description=
description=This is ideal for fresh servers, new characters, and multiplayer balance.
description=
description=[h1]Moon phase modifiers[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Moon phases [b]multiply[/b] the current risk.
description=
description=Example:
description=[list]
description=[*]Zone Risk: 20%
description=[*]Full Moon Modifier: 200 → will effectively double (200%) the risk to 40%
description=[*]New Moon Modifier: 50 → will effectively half (50%) the risk to 10%
description=[/list]
description=
description=Moon modifiers scale risk, they do not replace it. Unless you set it to 0, then it means there is no risk!
description=
description=[h1]What this update changes[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Every sprinter in the world now follows the same rules. Previously the game's sprinters and the mod's sprinters coexisted, and the mod could not slow a vanilla one down, so light suppression, dusk till dawn and zone limits silently did nothing to them. What you configure is now what you get.
description=
description=Speed is also set per zombie rather than by swapping a global setting, which removes a full stat re-roll on every change and makes zeds in the same light behave the same way.
description=
description=[b]Setting changes[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Replace vanilla sprinters with[/b] is new. It decides what the game's own sprinters become. Only used when Zombie Speed is Sprinters, and read once when the world loads.
description=[*][b]Percentage Sprinters Volume[/b] now works. The sprinter screech was reading the wrong setting, so this slider did nothing. Set it to 0 to silence the screech.
description=[*][b]Night Change Volume[/b] is now [b]Sprinting Change Volume[/b]. It plays when conditions change, not when a particular zed starts running, so with light suppression on you can hear it before you see anything move.
description=[*][b]Sprinter Skeletons[/b] has been removed. Aiming at a skeleton still crashes the client in B42, so the option is gone rather than left available to switch on.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Fixes[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Zeds already close to you now start and stop sprinting properly. One that was near you at dusk could previously keep running through the following day.
description=[*]A zone with its sprinter cap set to 0 now actually suppresses sprinters. The cap was being skipped at exactly that value.
description=[*]The first dusk or dawn sound of each session is no longer swallowed.
description=[*]Debug mode can be toggled without restarting.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Key differences to version 1
description=[list]
description=[*]Support for PhunZones 2
description=[*]Removed requirements for dependencies (PhunZones and PhunLib are no longer required)
description=[*]Unified the UI and core mods (UI is no longer a separate mod)
description=[*]Drop B41 support (current version of PhunSprinters will continue to work for B41 but this greatly simplifies things)
description=[*]Improve performance
description=[*]Reduce amount of "Why they no sprint" moments
description=[/list]
