title=PhunSprinters 2
description=
description=PhunSprinters is the ultimate dynamic sprinter experience for Zomboid. PhunSprinters does [b]not[/b] simply add sprinters.
description=It calculates [b]risk[/b] and that risk can change depending on [b]where you are, how long you've survived, the moon cycle, and the environment[/b].
description=
description=[h1]Why not just turn on vanilla sprinters?[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Vanilla gives you a percentage. Those zeds are sprinters everywhere, at all times, from your first hour to your last.
description=
description=PhunSprinters makes it situational. Risk climbs the longer you survive, shifts with the moon, and changes when you cross into a different area. Depending on the mode you pick, the same zed can shamble past you at noon and come at a dead run after dark.
description=
description=Then there is light. With suppression on, a sprinter caught in enough light drops back to a walk, which turns your torch from something you leave on into a decision you make.
description=
description=[h1]How risk is calculated[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Sprinter behaviour is determined by stacking modifiers in this order:
description=[list]
description=[*]Global Base Risk (in your settings)
description=[*]Zone Override (when [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3676252660]PhunZones 2[/url] is installed). [url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#zone-recipes]Recipes for safe havens and no-go areas[/url]
description=[*]How long you've played for
description=[*]What phase the moon is in
description=[*]The mode you use (see below)
description=[*]Light Suppression (enable to slow sprinters in light)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#how-risk-is-calculated]The exact formula, with a worked example[/url]
description=
description=[h1]Modes[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=[table]
description=[tr][th]Mode[/th][th]When sprinters run[/th][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]1- Dusk till Dawn[/b][/td][td]Walk during the day, run once the sun goes down[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]2- Always[/b][/td][td]Always run (like vanilla)[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]3- When it's too dark outside[/b][/td][td]At night, or when weather cuts visibility, even in daytime[/td][/tr]
description=[tr][td][b]4- When it's too dark around them[/b][/td][td]The phunest option, and the most demanding. Sprinters run whenever it gets too dark around them, so your flashlight becomes a critical part of gameplay[/td][/tr]
description=[/table]
description=
description=[h1]Light suppression[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=When enabled, light forces sprinters to slow down:
description=[list]
description=[*]Flashlights become tactical tools
description=[*]Base lighting matters
description=[*]Night travel becomes a risk vs reward decision
description=[*]Brings the Phun of mode 4 to modes 1, 2 and 3 (mode 4 already works this way)
description=[/list]
description=
description=[url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#light-suppression]How the light level is measured, indoors and out[/url]
description=
description=[h1]Sprinter speed[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Want sprinters without the instant death? [b]Sprinter Speed %[/b] slows them down, from 100 (vanilla) to 25. It slows the whole run, lunge included, and the zed genuinely covers less ground. Each sprinter keeps the speed it had when it started running, and only zeds this mod makes sprint are affected.
description=
description=[b]Note:[/b] this replaces the vanilla zombie sprint animation files, so don't run it alongside another "slower sprinters" mod.
description=
description=[url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#sprinter-speed]How it works[/url]
description=
description=[h1]Hours discount[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Ramps difficulty with time survived instead of throwing full risk at a fresh character. A new player can be seeing a fraction of an area's risk while a veteran standing next to them takes all of it. Ideal for fresh servers.
description=
description=[url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#hours-discount]The ramp, hour by hour[/url]
description=
description=[h1]Moon phase modifiers[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=Moon phases [b]multiply[/b] risk. A full moon night can be twice as dangerous as a new moon one, and every phase is configurable. Set a phase to 0 and there are no sprinters at all on those nights.
description=
description=[url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#moon-phases]Every phase and its default[/url]
description=
description=[h1]Sensor panel and sounds[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=A panel by the clock shows the moon and your current risk; hover it for the breakdown. Show it to everyone, or only to players wearing a lootable Military Sensor. Each player can turn the sprinter sounds down for themselves under Options > Mods.
description=
description=[h1]Vanilla Zombie Speed[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=PhunSprinters has to own who runs, so vanilla sprinters are converted to a slower speed when the world loads (on [b]Random[/b], only the sprinter share is touched). Nothing is written to your save. Want every zed running? Set Default Risk to 100 and Mode to Always.
description=
description=[url=https://github.com/PhunZoider/PhunSprinters#using-this-with-the-vanilla-zombie-speed-setting]The full breakdown, per Zombie Speed setting[/url]
description=
description=[h1]Compatibility[/h1]
description=[hr]
description=[list]
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3676252660]PhunZones 2[/url] for per-zone risk
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3792193021]PhunServer 2[/url] Day/Night drives dusk and dawn when installed
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3796669056]The Mutants[/url]: choose per mutant type whether it can become a sprinter. Leapers and Weepers are left to The Mutants by default
description=[*]Not compatible with other "slower sprinters" mods, which replace the same animation files. Use Sprinter Speed % instead
description=[/list]
